# AFTERDARK Product Requirements

## Product

AFTERDARK is an adults-only, private, exactly-two-player couples entertainment
application. It must not expose public profiles, public chat or random matching.

## Mandatory moods

- **PLATONIC**
- **ROMANTIC**
- **SEXUAL**
- **FANTASY**

The SEXUAL category name must not be renamed or removed.

## Core product areas

- Eighteen-plus confirmation.
- Anonymous nickname and partner setup.
- Private room creation and invitation.
- Pass & Play, Nearby and Anywhere play modes.
- Six Questions and alternate-role prediction games.
- Romantic, sexual, fantasy and Truth or Dare game engines.
- Private chat, reactions, replies and media.
- In-chat camera, gallery and voice notes.
- Trophy and achievement progression.
- Pair-specific, non-repeating content.
- Local persistence and offline recovery.
- Android packaging and distribution-specific controls.
